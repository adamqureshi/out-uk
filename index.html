Understanding the UK’s new EV discount

Useful context for buyers—especially on new and nearly-new EVs.

The UK has brought back a government-backed discount for certain new electric cars. Here’s the quick headline (details and eligibility can change):

  • Discount can be up to £3,750 off the purchase price
  • Qualifying EVs must be priced at or under £37,000
  • Scheme funding is set to run until 31 March 2029 (or until the money runs out)
